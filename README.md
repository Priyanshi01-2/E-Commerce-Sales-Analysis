# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify important sales trends, customer patterns, product performance, and business insights.

The analysis is performed using Python and focuses on data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), aggregation, and visualization.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Analyze sales by city
- Understand customer demographics
- Compare payment methods
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Study the relationship between quantity and sales
- Calculate Average Order Value (AOV)
- Generate actionable business insights

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Git & GitHub

## Dataset

The project uses an e-commerce sales dataset containing information about:

- Order Date
- Product Name
- Product Category
- Price
- Quantity
- City
- Gender
- Age
- Payment Method

## Analysis Performed

### Product Analysis
- Sales by Product Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales

### Customer Analysis
- Sales by Gender
- Sales by Age Group
- Sales by City

### Payment Analysis
- Sales by Payment Method

### Time-Based Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Yearly Sales Analysis

### Sales Metrics
- Sales by Quantity
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Category-wise Sales Distribution

## Key Insights

- Clothing and Home are among the higher-performing product categories.
- Kolkata records the highest total sales among the analyzed cities.
- Female customers contribute higher total sales than male customers in this dataset.
- The 46–55 age group records the highest sales among the analyzed age groups.
- Netbanking records the highest sales among the analyzed payment methods.
- Sales show noticeable month-to-month variation.
- Total sales increased from 2024 to 2025.
- The Average Order Value (AOV) is ₹2,428.30.
