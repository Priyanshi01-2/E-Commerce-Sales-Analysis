# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Python to identify sales trends, 
product performance, customer purchasing patterns, geographical performance, and payment preferences.

The analysis was performed using Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and data visualization 
techniques were used to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 2. Key Findings

### Product Category Performance
- Clothing and Home were among the highest-performing product categories.
- Electronics and Books also contributed significantly to overall sales.
- Toys generated comparatively lower sales.

### Top Products
- Curtains emerged as one of the highest-selling products.
- Lamp, Comics, T-Shirt, Fiction, and Smartphone also showed strong sales performance.

### City-wise Sales
- Kolkata generated the highest sales among the analyzed cities.
- Mumbai and Chennai also demonstrated strong sales performance.
- Delhi recorded comparatively lower sales.

### Customer Gender Analysis
- Female customers generated slightly higher sales than male customers.
- Both customer groups contributed substantially to total revenue.

### Payment Method Analysis
- Net Banking generated the highest sales among the analyzed payment methods.
- Credit Card and Cash on Delivery were also commonly used.
- UPI showed comparatively lower sales in this dataset.

### Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales varied significantly across different months.
- The highest sales level was observed around June 2025.
- Monthly trend analysis helps identify periods of high and low demand.

### Quantity Analysis
- Orders with quantity 1 generated the highest overall sales.
- Quantity 4 also contributed strongly to revenue.

---

## 3. Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important business opportunities:

- High-performing product categories can be prioritized for inventory and marketing.
- Strong-selling products can be promoted through targeted campaigns.
- High-performing cities can receive greater marketing investment.
- Low-performing regions can be investigated for growth opportunities.
- Customer purchasing patterns can support better audience segmentation.
- Payment preferences can help improve checkout and payment strategies.
- Monthly trends can support inventory planning and promotional campaigns.

---

## 4. Business Recommendations

1. Increase inventory for consistently high-performing product categories.
2. Promote top-selling products through targeted marketing campaigns.
3. Focus marketing efforts on cities generating higher revenue.
4. Investigate opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing cities.
5. Use customer demographic insights for personalized promotions.
6. Optimize payment options based on customer preferences.
7. Use monthly sales trends for demand forecasting and inventory planning.
